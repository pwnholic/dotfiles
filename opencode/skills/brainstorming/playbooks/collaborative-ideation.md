# Collaborative Ideation Playbook

Load for workshops, multiple human contributors, or explicitly authorized parallel-agent brainstorming.

## Authorization and Ownership

Do not spawn agents merely because parallelism might help. Delegation or parallel agent work must be authorized by the user or governing instructions.

Assign one coordinator to own:

- the shared decision frame;
- distinct exploration lanes;
- the coverage map;
- normalization of submissions;
- challenge and synthesis;
- the final decision artifact.

The coordinator must not erase unresolved disagreement.

## Compose for Cognitive Coverage

Choose contributors for distinct relevant knowledge, incentives, stakeholders, mechanisms, or time horizons. Where domain facts constrain feasibility, include qualified evidence or evaluation; perspective diversity cannot establish missing competence by itself.

Prefer situated perspectives tied to real knowledge or incentives. Treat stylized personas as unvalidated sampling prompts rather than proof of cognitive diversity.

Bad partition:

```text
everyone independently finds the best solution
```

Better partition:

```text
each contributor explores a different causal mechanism or neglected region
```

## Independent Generation

Before discussion or synthesis:

1. give every lane the same core frame and its distinct remit;
2. prevent exposure to the coordinator's preferred answer;
3. require assumptions and failure conditions with each idea;
4. let contributors report no viable option when a lane is exhausted;
5. normalize results into a common candidate schema.

For human groups, this limits turn-taking interference and evaluation pressure. For agents, it reduces shared-context anchoring. These mechanisms are different and should not be conflated.

## Synthesis Without Premature Convergence

The coordinator should:

- cluster by mechanism rather than wording;
- distinguish agreement from duplicated priors;
- preserve minority candidates with unique upside;
- mark overlaps, contradictions, and coverage gaps;
- launch a new independent lane for neglected regions when justified;
- mark failed or exhausted paths as blocked with reasons;
- combine candidates only after their standalone value is understood.

Do not use majority agreement as evidence of correctness or value.

More contributors or denser interaction is not automatically better. Use the smallest set of lanes that adds material coverage, and compare the expected information gain against coordination and compute cost. Multi-agent ideation should be able to justify what it adds beyond a diversified single-owner pass.

## Independent Challenge

Separate generation from evaluation. Where stakes justify it, assign critics who did not author the candidate to test:

- constraint violations;
- unsupported factual premises;
- hidden dependencies;
- failure modes and second-order effects;
- similarity to supposedly distinct ideas;
- evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

A critique should improve the decision, not reward adversarial tone.

## Iteration Rule

Start another round only when it has a defined purpose:

- fill a named coverage gap;
- resolve a consequential contradiction;
- test a critical assumption;
- repair a promising candidate;
- compare finalists under a changed scenario.

Stop when further rounds mostly repeat known mechanisms, critical gaps are explicit, and the next uncertainty is better resolved by evidence or user judgment.

## Human Workshop Adaptation

For human groups, use silent or asynchronous idea generation before open discussion when practical. Make contribution channels psychologically safe and avoid attributing early ideas when attribution would create hierarchy or evaluation pressure.

Restore accountability during evaluation and ownership assignment; anonymity should not obscure responsibility for consequential decisions.

## Deliverable

Return:

```text
shared frame
lane ownership and rationale
candidate map
unique versus overlapping contributions
blocked and neglected paths
independent challenges
coordinator synthesis
remaining disagreements and next decision
```
