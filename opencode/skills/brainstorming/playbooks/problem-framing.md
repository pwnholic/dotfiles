# Problem Framing Playbook

Load when the stated problem is ambiguous, contested, system-level, or dominated by uncertain assumptions.

## Frame Before Solving

Construct a compact frame:

```text
decision owner
affected actors
current condition
desired observable change
scope and horizon
hard constraints
assumed constraints
unknowns
decision required now
```

Do not convert a symptom, proposed feature, or favored solution directly into the problem statement.

## Separate the Inputs

Classify important statements as:

```text
observed fact
stakeholder goal
hard constraint
preference
assumption
unknown
proposed mechanism
```

Conflicting goals are design tensions to expose, not wording problems to hide.

## Generate Competing Frames

Reframe only along dimensions that can materially alter the solution space:

- actor: whose problem is it?
- outcome: what change actually matters?
- boundary: what is inside or outside the system?
- timescale: immediate relief, adaptation, or structural change?
- constraint: what if an assumed limitation is relaxed or inverted?
- cause: are we treating a symptom, bottleneck, incentive, or feedback loop?
- decision: what choice is reversible now, and what can wait for evidence?

Each candidate frame should imply a meaningfully different family of solutions.

## Test the Frames

Challenge each serious frame:

- Would success be observable?
- Does it center the correct actor and decision?
- Which evidence supports the causal story?
- What does it exclude?
- Which stakeholder would object, and why?
- Does it preserve the user's actual objective?
- Would a different frame reverse the preferred option?

Reject frames that are unfalsifiable, solution-shaped, or so broad that no decision follows.

## Decide What to Carry Forward

Select a primary frame when evidence and user intent support it. Otherwise carry a small set of competing frames into divergence and label the assumption behind each.

Do not force premature agreement. If one missing fact would choose between frames, propose the cheapest way to obtain it.

## Deliverable

Return:

```text
primary frame or frame portfolio
key actors and tensions
facts versus assumptions
scope and non-goals
unknowns that could change direction
decision or experiment that follows
```
